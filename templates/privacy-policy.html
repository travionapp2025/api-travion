Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 30, 2026

Travion (“we”, “our”, “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

Information We Collect

  • Basic profile information (name, email, phone number)
  • Travel details voluntarily provided for companionship matching
  • Device and app usage data

How We Use Your Information

  • To match you with compatible travel companions
  • To improve our services and user experience
  • To communicate important updates

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Limited data may be shared with trusted service providers solely for app functionality.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission is 100% secure.

Your Rights

  • Request access to your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Update inaccurate information

Data Collection and Usage

Signature Tech LLC collects profile information and travel-related data to facilitate itinerary sharing and related features within the Travion app.

Data Retention & Deletion (Guideline 5.1.1)

  • User Control: We give you full control over your shared data.
  • Deletion Process: You can delete your account and associated data via the "Delete Account" button on the Profile Screen.
  • Permanent Removal:Once deleted, personal information, itineraries, and chat history are permanently removed from our active databases.

Subscription & Payment Data (Guideline 3.1.2)

We do not store financial or credit card details. All subscription purchases and payments are handled securely by Apple through the App Store.

Safety and Moderation

We may use collected data to enforce safety and moderation policies. If a user is reported for violating our User-Generated Content (UGC) terms, we will investigate and take appropriate action within 24 hours.
